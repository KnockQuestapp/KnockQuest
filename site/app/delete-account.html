Request KnockQuest account deletion

KnockQuest lets you request deletion of your account and the associated cloud data we control. You do not need to sign in to submit a request.

Email a deletion request

What to include

  • Send the request from the email address associated with your KnockQuest account, where possible.
  • Write "Delete my KnockQuest account and associated data" in the message.
  • If you cannot send from that address, include the account email and ask us to verify your identity.

What happens next

We will verify that you control the account, then delete the account and active cloud records we control. We may retain limited information when required for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations, as described in the KnockQuest Privacy Policy. Backups may persist temporarily in routine recovery systems.

Data already sent to a connected CRM must also be deleted from that provider. Records saved only on your device or browser may need to be removed separately by clearing KnockQuest app or browser storage.

Questions about the request? Contact knockquest@gmail.com.
